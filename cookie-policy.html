Cookie Policy

Understand how cookies and similar browser technologies may be used when you access RP TOKEN.

Last Updated: 22 November 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how RP TOKEN may use cookies, browser storage and similar technologies when you visit or use our website and platform.

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store in your browser or device. They can help websites remember certain information, maintain sessions and support website functionality.

Similar technologies may include local storage, session storage and other browser-based mechanisms used to store or retrieve information.

2. Why We May Use Cookies or Browser Storage

Depending on the features enabled and the technical configuration of RP TOKEN, cookies or browser storage may be used for purposes such as:

  • Essential functionality: Supporting basic website and platform operations.
  • Login and sessions: Helping maintain user authentication and session-related information where supported.
  • User preferences: Remembering certain settings or preferences where implemented.
  • Platform operation: Supporting features that require information to be retained temporarily or between visits.

3. Types of Storage Technologies

The technologies used may vary according to the platform's actual configuration. They may include:

  • Session cookies or storage: Information retained during a browsing session and generally cleared when the session ends, depending on the technology.
  • Persistent cookies or storage: Information retained for a defined period or until removed.
  • Local storage: Browser-based storage that can retain information after a browser session ends.
  • Authentication-related storage: Information used by supported login or authentication functionality.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Some platform features may depend on third-party providers, such as hosting, authentication, payment or other technical service providers.

Depending on the services actually integrated and enabled, those providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

RP TOKEN does not claim that every third-party technology mentioned in this section is currently active on the platform.

5. Analytics and Advertising Cookies

Analytics or advertising technologies may be introduced only if such services are actually integrated and enabled on the platform.

This policy does not represent that RP TOKEN currently uses advertising cookies, behavioural advertising or any particular analytics provider.

If these technologies are introduced, this policy and any applicable consent mechanism will be updated as required.

6. How You Can Manage Cookies

Most modern browsers allow users to review, block or delete cookies through browser settings.

You may also be able to clear locally stored website data through your browser's privacy or site settings.

Please note that blocking or deleting certain essential cookies or browser storage may affect website functionality, login sessions or other platform features.

7. Consent and Your Choices

Where applicable law requires consent for particular cookies or similar technologies, the relevant consent should be obtained before those technologies are used.

The availability of a cookie notice, preference centre or consent-management feature depends on the platform's actual implementation. This policy alone does not create or operate a technical consent mechanism.

8. Data and Privacy

Information stored through cookies or similar technologies may be associated with your browser, device, account or platform activity, depending on the technology involved.

We aim to handle relevant information in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when platform features, technical integrations or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date. We encourage users to review it periodically.
