Refund & Cancellation Policy

Information about payment issues, cancellations and refund requests related to RP TOKEN.

Effective Date: 22 November 2026 | Last Updated: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains how payment-related requests concerning RP TOKEN may be handled.

RP TOKEN is an independently operated digital queue management platform. Certain features may be free of charge, while paid features or services may be introduced separately.

This policy applies where a payment has actually been requested or collected for a platform service. It does not create a payment obligation where no fee has been communicated.

2. Payment Information

Before making a payment, users should review the applicable service description, amount, duration, conditions and any specific cancellation terms displayed at the time of purchase.

Payments, where supported, may be processed through an independent third-party payment service provider. The payment provider may apply its own transaction procedures and timelines.

A payment confirmation from a payment provider does not necessarily mean that the requested platform service has been activated successfully. Users should verify the service status.

3. Duplicate Payments

If the same payment appears to have been charged more than once for a single purchase, please contact us with the relevant transaction details.

After reviewing the payment records and verifying the duplicate transaction, an eligible duplicate amount may be refunded or reversed through the appropriate payment channel.

4. Failed Payments and Amount Debited

Sometimes a payment may appear unsuccessful even though the amount has been debited from the user's bank account, card or payment instrument.

In such cases:

  • Check the transaction status with your payment provider.
  • Keep the transaction reference or payment ID.
  • Allow the payment provider's normal reconciliation process to complete.
  • Contact RP TOKEN if the amount remains debited but the service was not activated.

Refund or reversal timelines may depend on the payment provider, bank and applicable payment rules.

5. Cancellation Requests

If a user wishes to cancel a paid service, the request should be submitted through the official contact details listed on this website.

The eligibility and outcome of a cancellation request may depend on:

  • The type of service purchased.
  • Whether the service has already been activated or used.
  • The specific conditions disclosed before payment.
  • Whether the request is permitted under applicable law.

Any specific cancellation terms communicated at the time of purchase should be read together with this policy.

6. Refund Eligibility

Each refund request will be reviewed based on the circumstances and applicable terms.

Examples of situations that may qualify for review include:

  • A verified duplicate payment.
  • A payment collected for a service that was not provided.
  • A verified payment processing or activation error.
  • An incorrect charge attributable to a platform or payment issue.
  • Any other situation in which a refund is required by applicable law.

A refund will not be denied merely because a request is inconvenient to process. All requests will be assessed fairly, subject to the disclosed purchase conditions and applicable legal requirements.

7. Non-Refundable Situations

Subject to mandatory legal rights, a refund may not be available in certain circumstances, such as:

  • A service has been correctly delivered and fully used.
  • A user changes their mind after receiving the purchased service, where no cancellation right applies.
  • A problem results from incorrect information supplied by the user.
  • A service is unavailable due to circumstances attributable to the user's own device or network.

These examples are not intended to remove any statutory refund, cancellation or consumer protection rights available under applicable law.

8. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund review, contact us using the email address or WhatsApp number listed below.

Please provide the following information where available:

  • Your name.
  • Registered email address or mobile number.
  • Payment date and amount.
  • Transaction ID or payment reference.
  • Description of the issue.
Never send your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, password, OTP or full payment authentication credentials. These details are not required to investigate a refund request.

9. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated through the original payment method or another lawful method where appropriate.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the user's account may depend on the payment gateway, bank or financial institution.

Any estimated processing period communicated by the payment provider should be treated as an estimate unless the provider confirms otherwise.

10. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you notice an unauthorized or incorrect transaction, contact your bank or payment provider promptly and report the issue through their official dispute process.

You may also contact RP TOKEN so that the relevant transaction can be reviewed.

Nothing in this policy restricts your right to raise a valid payment dispute with your bank, payment provider or competent authority.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in payment arrangements, services or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with its revision date.

Any purchase-specific conditions already agreed with a user will be considered subject to applicable law and the terms communicated at the time of purchase.
