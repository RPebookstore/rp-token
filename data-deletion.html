Data Deletion

Information on how to request deletion of your RP TOKEN account and personal information.

Last Updated: 22 November 2026

At RP TOKEN, we respect users' privacy and provide a process through which users can request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

This page explains how to submit a deletion request, how requests may be verified and what information may need to be retained for legitimate legal or operational reasons.

1. Who Can Request Data Deletion?

The following users may submit a deletion request:

  • Registered customers using RP TOKEN.
  • Business owners or authorized business account users.
  • Individuals whose personal information has been submitted to the platform.

If you are submitting a request on behalf of another person or business, we may ask for reasonable proof that you are authorized to make that request.

2. How to Request Account or Data Deletion

To request deletion, contact us using the email address or contact details provided on this page.

Please include the following information in your request:

  1. Your registered name, if available.
  2. Your registered email address or mobile number.
  3. Your business name, if the request concerns a business account.
  4. A brief description of the account or information you want deleted.
  5. Whether you want full account deletion or deletion of specific information.
Suggested email subject: Request for Account and Data Deletion – RP TOKEN

3. Verification of Requests

To protect users and prevent unauthorized deletion, we may need to verify your identity or your authority over the relevant account.

Verification may involve confirming information associated with your account or contacting you through a previously registered communication channel.

We may ask for additional reasonable information if the request is unclear or cannot be safely verified.

4. What Information May Be Deleted?

Subject to verification, applicable law and technical limitations, a deletion request may cover information such as:

  • Registered account profile information.
  • Personal contact details associated with the account.
  • Business profile information submitted by the account holder.
  • Queue or token-related information associated with the user.
  • Other personal information that is no longer required to be retained.

The exact scope of deletion will depend on the information held, the account involved and applicable legal requirements.

5. Information That May Need to Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where there is a lawful and legitimate reason to do so.

Examples may include:

  • Records required by applicable law or regulatory obligations.
  • Information needed to resolve an ongoing dispute or complaint.
  • Records necessary for fraud prevention or security investigations.
  • Transaction or accounting records that must legally be maintained.

Where retention is required, the information should be limited to what is necessary and handled in accordance with applicable law.

6. Processing Time

We aim to review deletion requests within a reasonable period. The time required may depend on identity verification, the scope of the request, technical requirements and any legal retention obligations.

If additional information is required or processing is delayed, we may contact you using the details provided in your request.

Where deletion involves third-party service providers or backup systems, removal from those systems may take additional time according to their processes and applicable requirements.

7. Important Consequences of Deletion

Please note: Account deletion may result in loss of access to your account, profile, token history, queue-related records and other associated information. Some actions may be irreversible.

Before requesting deletion, please ensure that you have saved any information you are legally entitled to retain and may need for your own records.

8. Deletion of Specific Information

If you do not want your entire account deleted, you may request removal of specific personal information where applicable law permits.

We will review such requests based on the nature of the information, the purpose for which it is held and any applicable retention requirements.

9. Third-Party Services

RP TOKEN may rely on third-party providers for certain technical services, such as hosting, authentication, database storage or payment processing.

Where personal information is held by a third-party provider, deletion may also depend on that provider's systems, contractual arrangements and applicable legal obligations.

10. No Password or OTP Required by Email

Never include your account password, OTP, payment card number, PIN or other confidential authentication information in a deletion request.

We will not ask you to disclose your password or OTP through ordinary email or WhatsApp messages to process a deletion request.
