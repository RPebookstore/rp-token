Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and handled when you use RP TOKEN.

Effective Date: 22 November 2026 | Last Updated: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes the privacy practices applicable to RP TOKEN, an independently operated digital queue management platform.

This policy applies to visitors, registered users, customers and participating businesses who access or use the platform.

By using RP TOKEN, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this policy. Where consent is legally required for a particular processing activity, we will seek such consent separately where applicable.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the platform may process the following categories of information:

A. Account Information

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number, if requested.
  • Account identifiers and authentication-related information.

B. Business Information

  • Business or establishment name.
  • Business category and service information.
  • Business contact details and location, if provided.
  • Information required to configure a queue.

C. Queue and Token Information

  • Token numbers and queue status.
  • Customer-provided details required for queue management.
  • Visit, service and queue-related records.
  • Relevant timestamps and transaction records, if applicable.

D. Technical Information

  • Browser and device-related information.
  • IP address and technical request information, where available.
  • Session information and security-related logs.
  • Cookies or similar storage technologies, if used.

E. Communication Information

  • Messages and information sent to support.
  • Feedback, complaints and service requests.
  • Information voluntarily shared during communication.

We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for the relevant platform purpose. The actual information collected depends on the features and forms currently active.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for legitimate platform purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing digital token and queue management services.
  • Displaying queue status and relevant service information.
  • Maintaining platform functionality.
  • Responding to support requests and complaints.
  • Detecting misuse, unauthorized access and suspicious activity.
  • Maintaining records required for operational or legal purposes.
  • Improving platform performance and user experience.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We will not knowingly use personal information for a purpose unrelated to the reason it was collected unless permitted by applicable law or supported by an appropriate legal basis.

4. Legal Basis and Consent

Where required by applicable law, personal information will be processed on an appropriate legal basis, which may include consent, performance of a requested service, compliance with legal obligations or other legally recognized grounds.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent through an appropriate request. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

5. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

RP TOKEN may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers for hosting, database management, authentication, communications or other technical functions, depending on the services configured.

Such providers may process information on behalf of the platform in accordance with their applicable terms, privacy policies and contractual arrangements.

Third-party services may have their own privacy and security practices. Users should review the relevant provider policies where appropriate.

We do not claim that information is stored in a particular country or that a specific security certification applies unless that has been verified.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell personal information as a business activity. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary and legally permitted, including in the following situations:

  • With service providers supporting platform operations.
  • With a participating business where information is necessary to provide the requested queue service.
  • When required by a valid legal obligation or lawful government request.
  • To investigate suspected fraud, misuse or security incidents.
  • To protect the rights, safety and security of users or the platform, where legally permitted.

We do not authorize third parties to use information obtained through RP TOKEN for unrelated purposes.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain operational records, resolve disputes, enforce applicable terms or comply with legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, its purpose and applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, reasonable steps may be taken to delete, anonymize or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be maintained to help protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Depending on the platform configuration, safeguards may include access restrictions, authentication controls, secure communication and database permissions.

No website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their login credentials and avoid sharing passwords, OTPs or authentication codes.

Security measures may be updated as the platform develops. This policy does not represent that any specific security control, audit or certification has been completed unless separately confirmed.

9. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The platform may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to support login sessions, remember preferences, maintain functionality and improve the user experience.

The availability and use of such technologies depend on the actual website configuration and browser settings.

For further information, please read our Cookie Policy.

  • Requesting information about personal data processing.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of information where legally permitted.
